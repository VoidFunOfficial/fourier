# Mac mini · M6 — 75-second director's treatment

1920 × 1080 · 60 fps · 4500 frames · silent. Independent Fourier Scenes; each scene owns its motion. Root only composes scenes. No chapter headings, feature-card template, narrator, stock footage, simulated AI output, or permanent UI frame.

The visual sentence is a rounded square: pencil outline → aluminium enclosure → chip → core tiles → creative surfaces → ports → desktop → enclosure. Typography labels the thing being seen. Product lockups end with the requested handwritten ALL IN / MAC mini and production attribution. The palette is paper white, graphite, aluminium; muted peach, mint and periwinkle appear only as creative material. Light is a material property, never a decorative background wash.

## Polish decisions before implementation

- At most one primary claim per composition; secondary labels attach to geometry. No unrelated headlines or mini-headings.
- Accelerated movement is concentrated in approach, transformation and handoff. Important numeric endpoints remain readable for at least 1.4 seconds.
- The graphite line style has tiny deterministic irregularity, not continuous shaking of the product or text.
- Keep product joins as matched camera poses. Use geometric occlusion only where visual spaces change; avoid repeated generic fades.
- Text safe area 110 px horizontally, 80 px vertically. Product may deliberately crop during macro travel.
- Model is imported from the official Apple AR USDZ through Blender, retained as .blend with packed original textures, and exported as glTF/GLB, then animated through FourierCanvas. No Blender-rendered movie substituted for SDK motion.
- Reference videos could not be played in the available browser; transcript retrieval also failed. Only verified titles and user-requested style direction inform the treatment; do not claim frame-level reproduction of those films.

## Revision polish

- Runtime product geometry uses the official 74 meshes, with no decimation or redesigned ports. The former generic port spacing and horizontal USB-C openings are removed.
- Product leaders are 6 px, with round ends, and about 64–77 px long. The dimensions use separated short endpoint strokes rather than long spanning lines.
- The final scene is deliberately locked: 0.00 Productivity, 0.70 Creativity, 1.40 STEM, 2.10 Gaming, 2.80 Coding, holding through 4.00. The rest of the film remains fast moving.
- Fixed product region is compared pixel-for-pixel across six PNG samples, including all five workflows and final hold.

## Rhythm and handwritten ending revision

- The main product narrative is 62 seconds, down from 72, about 16% faster. Shortened scenes retime their full entrance, development and exit; 3D camera endpoints stay matched.
- The ending is 13 seconds: 4 seconds of continuous workflow cuts, 5 seconds of handwriting, then 4 seconds of requested production attribution. Total 75 seconds.
- Every visual remains an independent Scene. The three ending Scenes are composed by a small SDK Template so project compilation stays within the existing bounded worker queue. No SDK or engine safety limits were changed.
- Handwriting is a source snapshot of `fourier-sdk/example/HandWritingTextMotion.tsx`, with its original font and animation/filter implementation; the font import and named export are local; the reveal envelope is linear so writing-window offsets stay literal. Ink relief and deformation are reduced for a restrained finish.

## v4 sound direction and physical pen writing

Timing remains 75 seconds. The official five-workflow montage keeps the Mac mini fixed and places a short airy snap on each 0.7-second change. All preceding scenes retain the approved v3 motion. Their sound follows physical actions: sketch friction → enclosure air → dimensional clicks → chip/body impact → light compute ticks → layered media swipes → port latches → airflow → display placement. The 66–71s scene draws 29 actual SVG pen centre-lines. Pen contact starts at 66.133s, the final dot ends at 69.967s, then both ink and sound settle. Credits keep the requested text at 71–75s with two restrained arrival accents. No BGM.
